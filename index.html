How I grow pages

I don't guess. I read the algorithm.

Growth on Instagram isn't luck. It comes from knowing what the algorithm rewards and showing up with the right content at the right moment, every day.

🧠

Understanding the algorithm

I know what Instagram pushes: watch time, shares, saves and early engagement. Every post is built to hit those signals so it reaches people who don't follow the page yet.

📈

Spotting trends early

I watch trending audio, formats and topics every day and jump on them while they're rising. Being early is what turns a normal post into a viral one.

🎙️

Voice-overs that hold attention

Clear, punchy voice-overs that explain the story quickly and keep people watching to the end, which is exactly what the algorithm rewards.

🎯

The right content type

Reel, carousel, static meme or news update: I know which format works for which niche and which story, and I use the one that will travel furthest.

⏰

Posting at the right time

I post when the page's audience is most active, so every upload gets a strong start in its first hour, when reach is decided.

🪝

Hooks in the first 3 seconds

Strong opening lines, thumbnails and on-screen text that stop the scroll. If people don't stop, nothing else matters.

✍️

Captions, hashtags & SEO

Captions that start conversations, plus keywords and hashtags that help posts show up in search and Explore.

📊

Learning from the data

I check insights after every post (reach, shares, retention) and do more of what works. Each week's content is shaped by the last week's results.

⚡

Speed on breaking news

On news pages, being first matters. I turn breaking stories into clean, accurate posts within minutes, while everyone is searching for them.
